# Community Code of Conduct

ECP is committed to a respectful, precise, and welcoming technical community.
This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and claims rather than people.
- State uncertainty and technical boundaries honestly.
- Keep feedback specific, reproducible, and relevant to the proposed change.
- Respect privacy, disclosure embargoes, and the authorization boundaries of
  repositories and systems.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect project scope, safety, and
  community health, while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly false security, adoption, validation, or maintainer claims.
- Testing or targeting systems without authorization.
- Sustained disruption, spam, metric manipulation, or attempts to pressure
  participants into unsafe disclosure or external actions.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the public
contact method on the `Jack182617` GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive
personal details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities must use the
private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented when safe,
and focused on protecting people and the project.
